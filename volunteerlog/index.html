01 How hours are counted

1hour

Tutoring session

Per session you lead with a matched student.

2hours

Tutor referral

Per verified referral. See the requirements below.

½hour (30 min)

Family referral

Per verified referral. See the requirements below.

02 Referral requirements

When referral hours count

Referral hours are credited only after the person you referred reaches these milestones.

Tutor referral · 2 hours

The tutor you referred must complete all four:

  1. Complete onboarding

    Finish the full Tutor-For-All onboarding process and meet all program requirements.

  2. Pass qualifications review

    Have their academic qualifications (GPA and grades) verified by the team.

  3. Flash Power qualifications

    Show subject knowledge and readiness through the Flash Power qualification step.

  4. Tutor at least one session

    Run a full first session with a matched student.

Family referral · 30 minutes

The family you referred must complete:

  1. One completed session

    The student attends and finishes their first session with their assigned tutor.

03 Important

Keep your entries consistent, or we can't verify them

  • Full name: enter your first and last name exactly the same way every time. No nicknames, abbreviations or alternate spellings.
  • School name: type it identically on every submission. For example, always “Carnegie Vanguard High School”, never “Carnegie Vanguard HS” one week and “Carnegie Vanguard” the next. Inconsistent names delay or prevent verification.
