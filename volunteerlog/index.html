Volunteer hour log

Submit the form within 3 days of every tutoring session, and after any other qualifying activity. The Tutor-For-All team verifies each entry, and your hours can be used for NHS, scholarships and college applications.

01 How hours are counted

1hour

Tutoring session

Per session you lead with a matched student.

2hours

Tutor referral

Per verified referral. See the requirements below.

½hour (30 min)

Family referral

Per verified referral. See the requirements below.

02 Referral requirements

When referral hours count

Referral hours are credited only after the person you referred reaches these milestones.

Tutor referral · 2 hours

The tutor you referred must complete all three:

  1. Complete onboarding

    Finish the full Tutor-For-All onboarding process and meet all program requirements.

  2. Pass qualifications review

    Have their academic qualifications (GPA and grades) verified by the team.

  3. Tutor at least one session

    Run a full first session with a matched student.

They also need to put your name in the referral question when they apply.

Family referral · 30 minutes

The family you referred must complete:

  1. One completed session

    The student attends and finishes their first session with their assigned tutor.

The family also needs to put your name in the referral question on their sign-up form.

03 Important

Log on time and keep your entries consistent, or we can't verify them

  • Log within 3 days. If a session has no log, we'll email you to ask what happened. If you don't answer, it counts as an unexcused absence.
  • Full name: enter your first and last name exactly the same way every time. No nicknames, abbreviations or alternate spellings.
  • School name: type it identically on every submission. For example, always “Carnegie Vanguard High School”, never “Carnegie Vanguard HS” one week and “Carnegie Vanguard” the next. Inconsistent names delay or prevent verification.

05 Help with the forms

What each question asks

The hour log and the Report a problem form are in English. Here is what they ask, so you can fill them in with any level of English. In the Chrome browser you can also right-click the form and choose Translate.

Hour log

  • Your full name and your school, written the same way every time
  • What you are logging: a tutoring session, a tutor referral, or a family referral
  • Your student's first and last name
  • The date of the session
  • How long the session lasted, in minutes
  • What you covered and how it went
  • Any concerns: No, Yes - Minor, or Yes - Needs Follow-up
  • Notes for next time
  • Your TFA Board Snapshot, the picture that proves the session

Report a problem

  • I need to miss an upcoming session: you cannot come to a session. Tell us at least 24 hours ahead.
  • Session was held, I forgot to log it: the session happened but you did not log it in time.
  • Student did not show up: you came and your student did not.
  • The family cancelled this session: the family told you they could not come.
  • I had to cancel: you could not hold the session.
  • We rescheduled it: you moved one session to a new date. Give the new date and time.
  • We changed our regular day/time: your weekly session moves for good.
  • We added another weekly session: you agreed on an extra weekly time with the family.
  • We're taking a break: sessions stop until a date you choose.
  • The family has stopped coming: the family is not coming any more.
  • I need to stop tutoring: you are leaving. We tell the family and find them a new tutor.

Questions about your hours?

Missing hours, a referral that hasn't been credited, or a verification letter you need: email us and we'll sort it out.
