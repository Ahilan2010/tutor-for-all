Free tutoring for families

Free online tutoring for kids.

Tutor-For-All gives children from low-income families a real tutor, one-on-one, for free. No fees, no contracts, nothing to buy. All you need is an internet connection.

Request free tutoring →Common questions
Grades
Kindergarten to 5th grade
Subjects
Math, reading, or both
Format
One tutor, one child, online
Cost
$0. Always.

01 What you get

A tutor of your child’s own

  • One-on-one help. Your child is never in a group. The tutor plans around what they find hard.
  • About an hour a week. Same tutor, same time, at an hour that works in your time zone.
  • Math, reading, or both. From counting and letter sounds to fractions and long books.
  • Your language. We match your child with a tutor who speaks the language they speak best.
  • Reminders. We remind you before every session and you can sit in on any of them.

02 Who it is for

Families who can’t pay for a tutor

Tutor-For-All is for children in kindergarten through 5th grade from low-income households, in any country. The form asks a few confidential questions so our limited spots go to the families who need them most. Not sure if you qualify? Apply anyway.

Our tutors are high school and college students, not trained teachers. We can’t support children who need special education.

03 How it works

From sign-up to first session

1. Fill in the form. It takes about five minutes.

2. We review it and reply within 3 to 5 school days.

3. We find a tutor. When we have a match, you confirm it with one tap. How long this takes depends on how many tutors are free, so it can take a few weeks.

4. Sessions begin on Google Meet, on a laptop or tablet if you can. We send you the link and a calendar invitation.

04 Questions

Why is it free?

Our tutors are volunteers. They are students who want to help, and they earn verified service hours for it. Our small costs are covered by donations, so families never pay anything. Read our story, or see how we keep children safe.

An hour a week changes a lot.

The form takes five minutes. It is free, now and always.
