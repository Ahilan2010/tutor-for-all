Free math help

A free math tutor for your child.

A child who falls behind in math rarely catches up alone. Tutor-For-All gives children in kindergarten through 5th grade a patient tutor, one-on-one, for free.

Request free tutoring →
Grades

Format
One tutor, one child, online
Cost
$0. Always.

01 In a session

What a math session looks like

Your child and the tutor work problems together on our shared whiteboard, built for young children. The tutor starts with a quick review, teaches one skill, then watches your child try it and helps when they get stuck. Many tutors also set a little practice on Khan Academy, which is free and available in many languages.

02 Grade by grade

What we work on

GradeWhat we work on
KindergartenCounting, shapes, first addition and subtraction
1st gradePlace value, comparing numbers, measurement and data
2nd gradeMoney, time, basic geometry, adding and subtracting within 1,000
3rd gradeMultiplication, division, fractions, arithmetic patterns
4th gradeMulti-digit multiplication and division, fractions, area and angles, intro to decimals
5th gradeDecimals and fractions, algebraic thinking, volume, powers, line plots

03 Signs

When a math tutor can help

  • Your child counts on their fingers for simple sums.
  • Homework ends in tears.
  • They say “I’m bad at math.”
  • Their teacher says they are behind.

04 Try at home tonight

Five free ways to help

  • Count real things: stairs, spoons, coins.
  • Cook together. Halves and quarters make fractions real.
  • Shop together. Add up prices and work out the change.
  • Play. Card games and dice build number sense.
  • Practice a little, often. Ten minutes a day beats an hour on Sunday.
