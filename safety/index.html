For parents & guardians

How we keep children safe.

Your child’s safety comes first. Here is exactly what we do before a tutor meets a child, during every session, and if anything ever goes wrong.

01 Before a tutor meets a child

Every tutor is checked first

  • We review every application by hand. We check each tutor’s school, grades and reasons for tutoring.
  • Safety training. Every tutor completes our training and must get every safety question right on a quiz.
  • Code of Conduct. Every tutor agrees to our rules before they are matched with a child.
  • We see every tutor. Our team meets each new tutor on a short video call, or watches a short video that shows their face.
  • You confirm the match. You get an email with the match and choose whether to confirm it before anything is booked.

02 During every session

Sessions are closed, and you can always watch

  • Online only. Nobody comes to your home, and tutors never meet children in person.
  • Closed calls. Each Google Meet call is only for the people invited. Anyone else has to ask to join.
  • Parents welcome. A parent or guardian can sit in on any session, with no notice.
  • Cameras on. The tutor and the child keep their cameras on for the whole session.
  • No recording. Tutors never record a session. The only picture allowed is one end-of-session picture sent to Tutor-For-All as proof, and it is never shared.
  • Our whiteboard knows no names. The TFA Board asks for no child names, photos or accounts.

03 Rules for tutors

Clear limits on contact

  • Email between tutors and families always includes Tutor-For-All.
  • Phone numbers are for quick things on the day of a session, like running late.
  • No contact with a child on social media, and no following a family on personal accounts.
  • No gifts, and no meeting outside the online session.
  • A tutor who breaks these rules is removed from the program.

04 If something is wrong

Tell us right away

If anything about a tutor or a session worries you, email tutorforallnonprofit@gmail.com the same day, in any language. You can also stop tutoring at any time, with no reason needed.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Questions about safety?

We are happy to answer them before you sign up.
