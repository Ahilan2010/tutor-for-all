01 Who this is for

Families who can't pay for a tutor

Tutor-For-All is reserved for K–5 students from low-income households. Every session is online, so we work with families anywhere in the world.

The form asks a few short eligibility questions so our limited spots go to the families who need them most. Your answers are confidential and used only for screening.

Not sure if you qualify? Apply anyway, or email us.

02 Your child's safety

  • Vetted tutors. Every tutor meets our academic requirements and completes onboarding on how sessions run and how we keep students safe.
  • Code of Conduct. Every tutor agrees to it before they're matched with a student.
  • You can watch. Parents and guardians are welcome, and encouraged, to sit in on any session.
  • Online only. Sessions happen over Zoom or Google Meet. No one comes to your home.
  • Private. We never share your family's information with anyone outside Tutor-For-All.

03 After you sign up

What happens next

You'll hear from a real person on our team, usually within a few school days.

  1. We review

    We look over your request and confirm eligibility within 3–5 school days. We may send a quick follow-up question.

  2. We match

    We pair your child with a tutor by grade, subject and schedule, and by language if your child is learning English. Depending on how many tutors are free, this can take a few weeks.

  3. We schedule

    We email you your tutor's name, the day and time, and the video link, with a calendar invite, and a reminder the day before each session.

  4. Sessions begin

    About an hour each, focused on your child's math and reading goals. We keep you updated as they progress.

05 Questions

Parents often ask

The quick version. See every question on our FAQ page.

Does it really cost nothing?

Yes. There are no fees of any kind, now or later. Our tutors are volunteers, and donations cover our small costs.

Who are the tutors?

High school and college students with strong grades: at least a 3.5 GPA and a 90 or above in the subject they teach. Each one completes our onboarding and agrees to our Code of Conduct before being matched.

What does my child need?

An internet connection and a device with a camera, ideally a laptop or tablet. If you only have a phone, or your connection is unreliable, tell us on the form. We'd still like to hear from you.

My child is learning English. Can you help?

Yes. Tell us which language your child is most comfortable in, and we'll match them with a tutor who speaks it. That can take longer, because fewer of our tutors speak other languages.

How long does tutoring last?

As long as your child needs it. Some families want help for a few weeks before a test; others stay for a whole school year. The form asks for your best guess, and you can change your mind later.

Can I request a male or female tutor?

Yes, there's an optional question on the form. If you choose one, we only match your child with a tutor of that gender, which can mean a longer wait.

Not sure yet? Just ask.

If you have questions about eligibility, scheduling, or anything else, we're happy to talk it through before you sign up.
