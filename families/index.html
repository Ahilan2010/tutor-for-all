For parents & guardians

How tutoring works.

Everything that happens after you sign up, and the few things we ask of you. It takes about three minutes to read.

Can’t make a session? →
Cost
Free, always
Sessions
About 1 hour a week
Where
Online, on Google Meet
Can’t make it?
Cancel from the reminder email

01 Before the first session

From sign-up to your first session

  1. You sign up

    Fill in the short form. We reply within 3 to 5 school days.

  2. You wait for a tutor

    We look for a tutor who fits your child’s grade, subjects, language and times. After 2 weeks, and then about once a month, we email to ask if you still want a tutor. Please tap Yes within 3 days. Until you do, we can’t match your child.

  3. You confirm your tutor

    When we find a tutor, we email you their name and the day and time. Tap Confirm within 2 days, or the tutor goes to the next family.

  4. You get the intro email

    It has your tutor’s name and phone number, the weekly day and time on your clock, and the Google Meet link. A calendar invitation comes too. The first session is at least 2 days later.

02 Every week

What a normal week looks like

  • Same day, same time. About one hour, one tutor with your child, online. Nobody comes to your home.
  • Reminders. We email you the day before, and again shortly before the session starts.
  • Joining the call. Open the Meet link signed in to the Google account the invitation was sent to. If Meet says “Ask to join”, you are signed in to a different account: tap your picture at the top right and switch.
  • Be ready. A laptop or tablet with the camera on, in a quiet spot, a few minutes early.
  • You can watch. You are welcome to sit in on any session.
  • Homework. If your child is stuck on schoolwork, send it to the tutor with the homework link in your intro email.

03 If you can’t make it

Cancel from the reminder email

  1. Open the reminder

    We email you the day before every session.

  2. Tap “Cancel this session”

    Pick the session your child can’t make. You can also cancel one of the next few sessions there.

  3. That’s it

    Your tutor gets an email right away. A session you cancel does not count against you.

Please cancel at least 2 hours before the session starts

Later than that, text or call your tutor. Their number is in your intro email.

Need a few weeks off, or a different day or time? Reply to any of our emails and we will set it up.

04 Our attendance rule

Two missed sessions in a row

If your child misses 2 sessions in a row without cancelling, we email you. If we don’t hear back within 2 days, your spot goes to the next family on our waitlist.

You can ask to come back at any time, and we will put your child back on the waitlist.

If 3 sessions in a row are cancelled, we email to check that tutoring still works for you. Nothing ends without asking you first.

Why: our tutors are student volunteers who set this time aside for your child, and many families are still waiting for a tutor. Cancelling ahead keeps it fair for everyone.

05 Our emails

Watch for our emails

  • One address. Every email comes from tutorforallnonprofit@gmail.com, and the subject starts with “IMPORTANT”. Save the address so we don’t land in spam.
  • Tap the buttons. Most of our emails have a button. One tap is all we need: no forms, no accounts.
  • Your language. If you use another language, our emails come in that language with English underneath. You can reply in your own language.
  • How is it going? About a month in, we ask how tutoring is going. Your answer helps us get better.

06 Changes

Breaks, new times and stopping

  • School holidays. Before a holiday break, we email you if there is no session that week.
  • A new day or time. Agree it with your tutor first. We then email you the new time. If you did not agree to it, tap “That’s not right” in that email.
  • Stopping. Tap “No longer need tutoring?” at the bottom of any reminder email, or reply to us. Your child can join the waitlist again later.
  • Safety. Sessions are online only, calls are closed to anyone not invited, and tutors never record. How we keep children safe.

Still have a question?

Email us at tutorforallnonprofit@gmail.com, in any language.
