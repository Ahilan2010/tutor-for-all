01 Safety

The rules that keep children safe

This is the most important part. Four of the five questions at the bottom are about safety, and you must get all four right.

Keep your camera on

You and your student keep your cameras on for the whole session. If a student’s camera stays off for more than a minute, ask them to turn it on. If they refuse, end the session politely and email us right away.

Parents are always welcome

A parent or guardian should be at home and know the session is happening. A parent may sit in on any session with no notice. If a parent asks, always say yes.

Never record

Never record a session, and never take a picture of a student. The one exception is the end-of-session picture for your session log. Send it only to Tutor-For-All through the log form. Never keep it, post it, or show it to anyone.

Use only the link on the calendar invitation

Each student has one Google Meet link, on the calendar invitation. Use only that link. Never make a new link and never use your own. Join signed in to the Google account the invitation went to, or Meet will not let you in.

If a child says something worrying

If a child says or shows something that makes you worry about their safety, stay calm and keep tutoring. Do not ask probing questions and do not raise it with the parent. Tell Tutor-For-All the same day by email. Do not tell friends and do not post about it. Reporting laws differ from country to country, and we will guide you.

Keep contact inside Tutor-For-All

Copy tutorforallnonprofit@gmail.com on every email with a family. Use a phone only for quick things on the day, like running late. No social media with students or families. No gifts. Sessions are online only: never meet a family in person, even at a library.

You can always stop a session

If anyone says something that makes you uncomfortable, end the session and tell us. You will never be in trouble for that.

02 Sessions

How a session works

You do not write lesson plans. Every session follows the same simple plan. Five minutes of preparation is enough.

TimeWhat you do
0–5 minSay hello. Ask about their week and whether anything from last time is still confusing.
5–15 minReview last session with 2 or 3 quick problems or questions.
15–35 minTeach one skill. Show an example, then do one or two together.
35–45 minLet them practice alone while you watch. Help only when they are really stuck.
45–50 minSum up in one sentence, give a small challenge for next time, and end on something positive.

Teach on the TFA Board

The TFA Board is our whiteboard. It comes by email once you have a student. Open your link, pick your student’s Pairing ID, press Copy student link, and paste it in the Meet chat. Your student clicks it, with no login. Write, draw and paste problems on the board together.

Free tools in your language

Use Khan Academy for math. It is free and works in many languages: change the language at the bottom of its page. For reading, StoryWeaver has free children’s stories in hundreds of languages. Never pay for anything.

Be kind and patient

Praise what they did: “You worked that out carefully.” Ask questions instead of giving answers. If something is not clicking, try a different way, not a louder voice. Never show frustration.

After every session

Log the session the same day, and always within 3 days. Press Snapshot on the board and upload the picture with your log. A session with no log counts against you.

If you cannot come

Fill in the Report a problem form at least 24 hours before the session. We tell the family for you. If your student does not come, report it on the same form. Do not log a session that did not happen. Three unexcused absences in six months end your tutoring.

03 Code of Conduct

What you agree to

  1. I will treat every student with patience, kindness, and respect, whatever their level, background, or behavior.
  2. I will show up on time and prepared for every session.
  3. I will keep my camera on for the whole session and ask my student to do the same.
  4. I will communicate professionally with parents and guardians, and reply to messages within 24 hours.
  5. I will log every session the same day if I can, and always within 3 days.
  6. I will log my hours honestly. I will not inflate session times, fake referrals, or send proof for something I did not do.
  7. I will never record a session. The only picture I make is the end-of-session picture for my session log, and I send it only to Tutor-For-All.
  8. I will never share student or family contact details, names, photos, or personal details with anyone outside Tutor-For-All.
  9. I will not contact a student or their family on social media.
  10. I will not give gifts to, or accept gifts from, student families.
  11. I will report any safety concern to Tutor-For-All right away.
  12. I will not discriminate against anyone because of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, gender, sexual orientation, or anything else.
  13. If I need to stop tutoring for any reason, I will tell Tutor-For-All and the family in advance. I will not disappear.
  14. I understand that breaking these commitments can remove me from Tutor-For-All, and that no hours are given for sessions that are unverified or false.
