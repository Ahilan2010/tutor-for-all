About Tutor-For-All

A child's zip code shouldn't decide how well they read.

We're a student-led nonprofit. High schoolers tutor elementary kids from low-income families, one-on-one, for free. That's the whole idea, and we take it seriously.

Founded by
Students at Carnegie Vanguard High School, Houston
Who we serve
K–5 students from low-income families
Format
One-on-one, online, about an hour
Cost to families
Nothing, ever

01 Where we started

We saw the gap from a few miles away.

Tutor-For-All began with students at Carnegie Vanguard High School in Houston, a city where roughly four out of five students in the public school district come from low-income households.

We kept seeing the same thing: families who wanted to help their kids but couldn't afford $40 to $80 an hour for a private tutor, and elementary students falling behind in math and reading. Not because they weren't capable, but because nobody had time to sit with them early enough.

So we started sitting with them. We recruit strong high school students to tutor younger kids, and everyone comes out ahead: the child gets free one-on-one help, and the tutor gets real teaching experience, verified volunteer hours, and the satisfaction of watching something click.

03 Milestones

How we've grown

Small team, steady progress. Here's what's changed since we started.

  1. 2025

    The first sessions

    Students at Carnegie Vanguard start tutoring K–5 kids over video call in math and reading.

  2. Spring 2026

    About 500 books, each with a sticker

    With Books Between Kids, we put roughly 500 books into the hands of students who need them. Every book carries a Tutor-For-All sticker so families know free tutoring exists.

  3. Summer 2026

    Our first supporters, and a real hour log

    Kindred Stories and The Book Readers Venue become our first financial supporters. We launch a volunteer hour log so every tutor's service is tracked and verified.

  4. Fall 2026 · Now

    Open to more tutors, in more places

    Applications reopen after a summer pause. Every session is online, so tutors and families can now join from any time zone. We updated tutor requirements to a 3.5 GPA plus a 90 in the subject you tutor, and built a self-paced onboarding course.

04 The book & sticker campaign

Every book is also an invitation.

Access to books at home is one of the strongest predictors of reading success, and many of the kids we serve have very few. So alongside tutoring, we collect and give away books with help from partners like Books Between Kids and Kindred Stories.

Each book carries a Tutor-For-All sticker that tells parents free tutoring is available. It reaches families right when they're thinking about their child's reading, which is exactly when a tutor can help most.

Have books or supplies to give? See what we accept on the donate page.

05 What we believe

Four commitments

Every tutoring hour, every donated book, and every volunteer goes to K–5 students from low-income families. Here's how we run things.

Start early

We focus on kindergarten through 5th grade because gaps in early reading and math are hardest to close later.

Free means free

No fees, no deposits, no waitlist that depends on what you can pay. If your family qualifies, we'll find a way to help.

Students teaching students

Our tutors are only a few years removed from the material. They remember what was confusing, and kids relate to them.

Safe and accountable

Tutors meet academic standards, complete onboarding on student safety, and sign our Code of Conduct. Parents can watch any session.
