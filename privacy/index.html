Privacy notice

Your information, in plain words.

This notice explains what Tutor-For-All collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. It applies to families, tutors and visitors in every country.

Last updated
November 6, 2026
Contact
tutorforallnonprofit@gmail.com

01 What we collect

The information you give us

  • Families: your child’s name, grade, school, subjects, language and what they find hard; your name, email, phone number, country and time zone; the times that work for you; and eligibility answers such as income range and assistance programs.
  • Tutors: your name, contact details, school, grades, languages, availability, your training results, a short video if you send one, and your session records and hours.
  • Sessions: dates, attendance and short notes from tutors, and homework a family chooses to send. Our whiteboard keeps a record of when a session happened, without names.
  • Website visitors: Google Analytics tells us which pages are visited and roughly from where. Visitors in Europe and the UK are not tracked with cookies.
  • Donors: PayPal handles donations. We see your name, email and the amount, not your card details.

02 Why we use it

To run the program, and nothing else

  • To check eligibility, so our limited spots go to the families who need them most.
  • To match each child with a tutor, and to schedule and remind you about sessions.
  • To verify volunteer hours, and to keep children safe.
  • To report total numbers to donors and partners. These totals never name a child or a family.
  • We never sell your information, and we never use it for advertising.

03 Who can see it

A small team, your match, and the services we rely on

  • Our team and any helpers we add, who see only what their job needs.
  • Your match. A tutor sees the child’s name, grade, school, what they need help with, and the language they speak. A family sees the tutor’s name and school. Both get each other’s email and phone number for scheduling. Tutors never see income or eligibility answers.
  • Google runs our forms, spreadsheets, email, calendar, video calls, translation and website statistics.
  • Cloudflare runs our whiteboard, GitHub hosts this website, and PayPal handles donations.
  • We share information with others only if the law requires it, or to protect a child from harm.

04 Where it is kept

Stored in the United States

Tutor-For-All is based in the United States, and the services above store information on servers in the United States and other countries. If you live somewhere else, your information leaves your country when you use our forms. We ask for your agreement to this on each form.

When we write to you in your language, the text of the email passes through Google Translate.

05 Children

Parents and guardians stay in charge

A parent or legal guardian fills in the family form. We do not ask children to give us information or create accounts with us. If you think a child has given us information without a parent’s knowledge, tell us and we will remove it.

06 Your choices

You can ask us to see, fix or delete your information

Email tutorforallnonprofit@gmail.com to see what we hold about you or your child, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. You can leave the program at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

We keep information for as long as we need it to run the program and to confirm volunteer hours. Whiteboards are deleted after a few days. Tutors may need their hour records for years, so we keep those unless you ask us to delete them.

Questions about your privacy?

Write to us in any language and a real person will answer.
