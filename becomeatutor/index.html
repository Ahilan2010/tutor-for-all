02 Why tutor with us

More than a line on your resume

Though it's a very good line on your resume.

A college essay waiting to happen

Admissions officers look for sustained, meaningful service. Teaching one kid, week after week, and watching them improve is exactly that.

Real leadership

Explaining fractions to an eight-year-old builds patience and communication faster than any club meeting.

Master your own material

The fastest way to understand something is to teach it. You'll be sharper in your own classes.

Direct, visible impact

You'll be helping a kid who otherwise wouldn't have a tutor, and you'll see the difference yourself.

Tutor in your own language

Speak Spanish, Vietnamese, Hindi, or something else? You could be the perfect match for a child who speaks it. You do not need to speak English to tutor with us.

A reference from us

Active tutors can request a reference letter from our team for internships, jobs, and college applications.

03 Who can apply

Requirements

We hold a high bar because our students deserve great tutors. We updated these for fall 2026.

Not quite there yet?

If you're close, apply anyway. We review every application individually, and if it's not a yes this time, you're welcome to apply again next semester.

  • HS or college
    Currently enrolled in high school or collegeHigh school students in grades 9–12 and college or university students are both welcome. Sessions are online, so you can tutor from anywhere in the world.
  • 3.5 GPA
    Minimum unweighted GPAAt least a 3.5 on a 4.0 unweighted scale, or the same level in your country's grading system.
  • 90+
    In the subject you tutorTutoring math? You need a 90 or above in your current or most recent math course. Reading? The same in your language class. Both? We look at both. Does your school grade differently? Tell us your grades your way on the form.
  • 1–2 hrs
    Each weekScheduled around your availability. Consistency matters more than volume.
  • Online
    Reliable internet and a deviceA computer or tablet you can use for video sessions.
  • Language
    English, or another languageYou do not need English to tutor with us. We match you with children who speak your language, and you can do your training in that language too.

04 What happens next

Application to first session

Four steps. Most of it happens on your own schedule.

  1. Apply

    Fill out the form below: your grades, subjects, availability, and why you want to tutor. It takes about five minutes.

  2. We review

    Our team checks your application and grades, then contacts you within 3–5 school days.

  3. Onboarding

    Short training videos, then a short form: five questions on how sessions work and how we keep students safe, our Code of Conduct, and your availability. Prefer another language? You can read the whole training and take the quiz in your language instead. Then a quick hello video call, or a short video of yourself.

  4. Get matched

    We pair you with a K–5 student by grade, subject, schedule and language. Sessions happen over Google Meet.
